ELECTRIC EEL PRIVACY POLICY

Effective date: September 28, 2026

Electric Eel is a free, open-source media client for Android TV, published by
the Electric Eel project (https://github.com/cygnusx-1-org/electriceel) ("we",
"us"). It is based on SmartTube and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or
sponsored by Google or YouTube.

This policy covers the official Electric Eel builds:
org.cygnusx1.electriceel.stable and org.cygnusx1.electriceel.beta. Builds made
by other people from the source code, and the SmartTube app it is based on, are
outside it.


IN SHORT

- We run no servers. Nothing you do in Electric Eel is sent to us, and we hold
  no data about you.
- Electric Eel contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash reporting,
  and sets no cookies of its own.
- Your settings, sign-ins and YouTube Data API key are stored on your TV, in the
  app's private storage.
- The app talks directly to the services it needs: mainly Google and YouTube,
  plus a few optional community services described below.
- Electric Eel uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service
  (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy
  (https://policies.google.com/privacy).


1. GOOGLE USER DATA

Electric Eel can connect to your Google account in three separate ways. Each one
is optional, each one asks for your consent on its own, and each one can be
revoked on its own.

1.1 Getting your YouTube Data API key ("Get a key automatically")

Some features, for example hiding videos from Home by their category or topic,
work better with a YouTube Data API key. Electric Eel can find or make that key
in your own Google Cloud account, so you don't have to make one by hand.

When it runs. Only on devices with Google Play services, and only when you start
it: from "Get a key automatically" in the "API keys" settings, or when the app
offers it right after you sign in to YouTube (it is offered only if you have no
key yet). You can cancel at any point, and you can always type a key in by hand
instead.

What access it asks for. Google Play services shows its own account picker and
consent screen, asking you to allow Electric Eel to "see, edit, configure and
delete your Google Cloud data" (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/cloud-platform).
Google only offers this access in that broad form. Electric Eel uses it only for
the steps below.

What it does with that access, in this order:

1. Looks for a key you already have. It lists the Google Cloud projects your
   account can see (their ID, name, labels and state). In up to 50 of them,
   starting with a project it made for you before, it lists the API keys (their
   name, display name and restrictions) and checks whether the YouTube Data API
   is turned on. For a key that can call the YouTube Data API from a TV app (one
   with no website, IP address, Android or iOS restriction), it reads the key's
   value and makes one test request to the YouTube Data API with it. The first
   key that works is used. Keys that don't work are discarded and not stored.
2. Only if there is no such key, makes one:
   - it reuses the project it made for you before, or creates a new project
     named "SmartTube YouTube Data API", with an ID starting "smarttube-yt-" and
     the label "smarttube-data-api";
   - it turns on the YouTube Data API in that project;
   - it reuses the key named "SmartTube YouTube Data API" in that project, or
     creates one with that name, restricted to the YouTube Data API, and reads
     its value;
   - it tests the key with one YouTube Data API request.

The names match the SmartTube key web page, so a project or key made by one is
found and reused by the other instead of being made twice.

What it never does. It does not change or delete any project, key or setting
other than the project it created, it does not turn on billing, and it does not
read any other Google Cloud data (for example storage, compute, databases,
permissions or billing information).

What is stored, and where:

- The access token Google issues for this is held in the app's memory only while
  the steps above run. The app never writes it to storage and never sends it
  anywhere except Google's APIs. Google Play services keeps your consent until
  you revoke it.
- The API key that was found or made is stored in the app's private storage on
  your TV, and in any backup of the app's settings that you make (see section
  3).
- The project and key stay in your Google Cloud account until you delete them.

Who receives it. Only Google: cloudresourcemanager.googleapis.com,
serviceusage.googleapis.com, apikeys.googleapis.com and www.googleapis.com (the
YouTube Data API). We never receive your access token, your list of projects or
your API key.

These requests go through our Google Cloud OAuth client, so Google may show us
aggregate statistics about it, such as how many users have granted access and
how many requests were made. Those statistics don't identify you or show us your
projects or keys.

1.2 Using your YouTube Data API key

With a key set, Electric Eel sends the IDs of videos it is about to show to the
YouTube Data API (videos.list), asking only for each video's category and
topics. It uses them to apply your content filters, and caches the result on
your TV by video ID. These requests are made with your key, so they count
against the free daily quota of the project the key belongs to. Electric Eel
doesn't turn on billing, so they cost you nothing.

1.3 Signing in to YouTube

Signing in is optional. You sign in by entering a code on Google's own page on
your phone or computer; Electric Eel never sees your password. The access this
grants appears in your Google account's list of third-party connections, where
you can remove it (see section 4).

The app stores the resulting sign-in tokens, and your account's name, email
address and profile picture, in its private storage on your TV. It uses them to
show your subscriptions, playlists, history and recommendations, and to carry
out actions you ask for, such as subscribing, liking a video or adding it to a
playlist. That information is exchanged only with YouTube and Google.

1.4 Backing up to Google Drive

Backing up to Google Drive is optional. It asks for access only to the files
Electric Eel creates in your Drive (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file);
it cannot see your other files. It uploads an archive of the app's settings to a
"SmartTubeBackup" folder in your own Drive, and reads it back when you restore.
That archive includes your YouTube sign-in and your YouTube Data API key. It is
stored only in your Drive; we have no access to it.

1.5 Limited Use

Electric Eel's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any
other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy
(https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including
the Limited Use requirements. In particular, data from Google APIs is:

- used only to provide the features described above, which you can see in the
  app;
- never transferred to anyone, except to Google as needed to provide those
  features, or as required by law;
- never used for advertising, never sold, and never used to decide whether you
  are creditworthy or for lending;
- never read by a human;
- never used to develop, improve or train generalized artificial intelligence or
  machine learning models.


2. OTHER SERVICES ELECTRIC EEL CONTACTS

Besides Google and YouTube, Electric Eel contacts the following services
directly from your TV. Like any website you visit, each of them sees your IP
address and the request itself. None of them receives your Google account,
sign-in tokens or API key. Each is run by someone else and governed by its own
privacy policy.

- SponsorBlock (sponsor.ajay.app), when SponsorBlock is on: the ID of the video
  you are watching, to fetch segments to skip.
- DeArrow (sponsor.ajay.app, dearrow-thumb.ajay.app), when DeArrow is on: the
  IDs of videos being shown, to fetch community titles and thumbnails.
- Return YouTube Dislike (returnyoutubedislikeapi.com), when "Show
  likes/dislikes count" is on: the ID of the video you are watching.
- AiSList (raw.githubusercontent.com), when the AiSList filter is on: nothing
  about you; it downloads public block and warn lists.
- GitHub (github.com), when the app checks for updates or needs its
  configuration: nothing about you; it downloads update information and the
  app's configuration file.
- Google Public DNS (8.8.8.8, dns.google), only if "Prefer Google DNS" is on:
  the names of the servers the app connects to.

Links in the app, such as to the project page or to donation sites, open only
when you choose them. Once you leave the app, the site you visit is governed by
its own policy.


3. DATA ON YOUR TV AND IN BACKUPS

Everything Electric Eel keeps (settings, filters, watch positions, cached video
categories, your YouTube sign-in and your YouTube Data API key) is stored in the
app's private storage on your TV. Other apps cannot read it, and it is deleted
when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

The app writes diagnostic messages to Android's system log, which stays on your
TV. The Google Cloud steps in section 1.1 never write your access token or your
API key to it.

If you make a local backup, the app writes an archive of its settings to your
TV's shared storage (a "SmartTubeBackup" folder in Documents on recent Android
versions). Like the Google Drive backup, it contains your YouTube sign-in and
your YouTube Data API key, and other apps with storage access on that TV can
read it. Keep backups somewhere private, and delete old ones you no longer need.


4. YOUR CHOICES, AND DELETING YOUR DATA

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, export
or delete on request. You control everything yourself:

- Revoke Electric Eel's access to your Google account: open your Google
  account's third-party connections page
  (https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions) and remove
  Electric Eel, or the YouTube or Google Drive connection it made. The access
  stops at once.
- Remove the API key from the app: turn off "YouTube Data API key" in the "API
  keys" settings.
- Delete the project and key from your Google account: open the Google Cloud
  console (https://console.cloud.google.com/) and delete the "SmartTube YouTube
  Data API" project, or just its key. Deleting the project deletes the key too.
- Sign out of YouTube: in the app's account settings.
- Delete everything on the TV: uninstall Electric Eel, or clear its data in
  Android's app settings.
- Delete backups: delete the "SmartTubeBackup" folder from your Google Drive and
  from your TV's storage.


5. SECURITY

Electric Eel connects to every service over HTTPS. Sign-in with Google happens
on Google's own pages and screens, so the app never sees your password. Tokens
and keys are kept in the app's private storage, and we never have a copy of
them.


6. CHILDREN

Electric Eel is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect information from anyone. Using YouTube through Electric Eel is subject
to YouTube's own age requirements.


7. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If the app starts handling your data in a new way, this policy will be updated
before that version is released, and the effective date above will change. The
full history of this policy is public in the project's repository.
